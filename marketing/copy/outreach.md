# Creator and newsletter outreach (drafts)

Send from L−14. Personalise the first line every time; mass-sent pitches get ignored. Offer a free Pro key and,
optionally, a 30% affiliate link through Lemon Squeezy's affiliate program. Never ask for a positive review, and if
they post, they should say they got a free key (UK ASA/CAP rules and the FTC both require disclosure of gifted
products).

## Who to look for

- **Obsidian / PKM YouTubers and bloggers** (group 2). Angle: the Dataview-friendly file and the vault workflow.
- **Journaling creators** on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok (group 3). Angle: owning your journal, no subscription.
- **Indie-dev and productivity newsletters** (group 1). Angle: one HTML file, no server, honest pricing.
- **Developer podcasts** that feature side projects.

Keep a simple sheet: name, channel, audience size, why they fit, date contacted, reply, posted (link).
